Lomo Saltado

Home

It is one of the most popular dishes in Peru.

The sauteed loin is a typical dish of Peruvian cuisine consisting of beef, cooked rice and chips.

Ingredients

  • beef loin
  • red onion
  • tomatoes
  • yellow pepper
  • cloves of garlic
  • chips
  • Soy sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Cut the beef tenderloin into thin strips.
  2. Fry the loin in a hot pan with oil until golden.
  3. Add chopped onion, tomato, yellow chili and garlic. Sauté for a few minutes.
  4. Add soy sauce (soya or sillao), vinegar and a little chopped coriander.
  5. Add fries and mix well.
  6. Cook for 2-3 minutes to mix the flavors.
  7. Serve hot with white rice.
