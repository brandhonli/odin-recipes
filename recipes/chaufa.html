Chaufa

Home

Chaufa is a type of fried rice consumed in Peru.

It is part of the tusan gastronomic style, being part of which is called as chifa cuisine. It is the most representative dish of this kitchen.

Ingredients

  • cups of cooked rice (preferably cold)
  • chicken or pork breast (in strips)
  • eggs
  • Chinese onion (or chive) chopped
  • tablespoons of soy sauce
  • tablespoon sesame oil (optional)
  • teaspoon of chopped ginger
  • carrot (optional, in thin strips)
  • Oil for frying
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Fry the protein: Cook the chicken or pork in a pan with a little oil until golden.
  2. Make the scrambled eggs: In the same pan, add the beaten eggs and stir until cooked.
  3. Saute the vegetables: Add the Chinese onion, carrot and ginger. Cook for a few minutes.
  4. Add the rice: Stir in the cold rice and stir well.
  5. Season: Add soy sauce, sesame oil, salt and pepper to taste.
  6. Mix everything: Stir everything well so that the flavors are mixed.
