The carapulcra is a typical stew of the gastronomy of Peru

It is a dish linked to the mixing of indigenous and Afro-Peruvian culinary cultures, one of the oldest known in the Andean country.

Ingredients

  • onion
  • dried potato
  • peanut
  • loin meat
  • garlic
  • chicken broth
  • yellow pepper

Steps

  1. Hydrate the dried potato in warm water for 4-6 hours.
  2. Fry the meat (pork or chicken) until browned and reserve.
  3. Fry onion, garlic, chili, yellow pepper and tomato until well cooked.
  4. Boil the hydrated potato for 20-30 minutes until soft.
  5. Shred or crush the cooked potato.
  6. Add the meat to the sauce with broth, bay leaf and shredded potato.
  7. Cook everything together for 15-20 minutes on low heat.
  8. Season with salt and pepper.
  9. Serve hot with white rice and fried banana (optional).
